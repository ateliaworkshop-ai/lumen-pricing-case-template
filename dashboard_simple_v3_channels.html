How it is calculated
Trial customers × conversion = initial customers. Initial customers × repeat rate = repeat purchases. Initial customers + repeat purchases = estimated units. Revenue = price × units. Contribution = supplied contribution/unit × units. Acquisition cost = converted customers × CAC. Final result = contribution − acquisition cost − launch budget. Break-even units = launch budget ÷ contribution available per initial customer after CAC, using the assumed repeat rate.
05 · Advice

Most successful strategy

Balanced Launch at €2.19
Start with DTC Online and Gym & Office. The case evidence supports a middle-price test because acceptance remains materially higher than the premium option, while these two channels preserve stronger contribution than Retail / Grocery. This makes the recommendation a combined price + distribution decision, not a price decision alone.
Downside: it gives up some unit margin versus Premium and some customer acceptance versus Fast ROI, so it is the compromise rather than the maximum on either dimension.
€1.79 Fast ROI — choose when: management explicitly prioritises trial/adoption over unit economics.
Downside: contribution per unit is the lowest of the three strategies, so more units are required to recover the same launch investment and the lower price may weaken premium positioning, and Retail / Grocery delivers thinner contribution than DTC or Gym & Office.
€2.59 Premium — choose when: management has evidence that premium willingness-to-pay is stronger than the current survey acceptance suggests.
Downside: supplied survey acceptance falls to 26.7%, creating the greatest risk of limiting trial volume and slowing adoption; using only DTC would also leave less room for the targeted Gym & Office trial channel.
Supplied evidence Survey acceptance and contribution figures come from the supplied LUMEN case/data. Model assumptions Trial volume, conversion, repeat rate, CAC and launch budget are user-editable assumptions. This simulator compares scenarios; it is not a German sales forecast.

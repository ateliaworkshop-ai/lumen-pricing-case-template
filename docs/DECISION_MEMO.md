# Decision memo

**To:** Freya Lindqvist, Head of Growth  
**From:** LUMEN Strategy & Analytics  
**Subject:** Germany launch price and first channels  
**Date:** 14 September 2026

Freya,

We recommend launching LUMEN in Germany at **€2.19 per can through DTC Online and Gym & Office**, beginning with a measured Berlin/Munich pilot. This is the best first move for balancing the CMO's premium positioning ambition with the CFO's need to recover marketing investment quickly.

The simulator makes the trade-off explicit. At €2.19, the selected DTC/Gym & Office mix produces approximately **€1.16 weighted contribution per can** and a **6.0-month CAC payback** under the default comparability and ramp assumptions. It also preserves a credible position near premium-performance competitors: below VoltFit in Gym & Office and materially below Root & Rise. DTC gives LUMEN control of the customer relationship and repeat-purchase data, while Gym & Office provides a relevant point of need and avoids the distributor cut.

The volume-first alternative—**€1.79 through Retail/Grocery**—has the stronger tested acceptance rate (**61.7% versus 51.7%** at €2.19), but its retail and distributor deductions leave only about **€0.63 contribution per can** in the simulator. That pushes CAC payback to roughly **12 months**, about twice as long as Option A, before we have German evidence that the extra reach will convert into repeat purchase. The €2.19 choice therefore deliberately gives up some top-of-funnel volume and broad retail reach to protect cash recovery, contribution, and brand meaning. It is not a claim that Retail should never be used; it is a sequencing decision.

Run the first six months as an instrumented pilot. Set targets for conversion, repeat purchase, contribution after fulfilment, channel-level CAC, and payback, and review them monthly against the adjustable comparability and ramp assumptions. Use the pilot to test creative and sampling with wellness professionals, capture actual DTC cohorts, and secure Gym & Office points of sale. Add Retail/Grocery only when observed repeat and margin support the €2.19 economics—or introduce €1.79 selectively as a controlled acquisition tactic with a clear limit. Keep forecasts as ranges, reconcile the supplied home-market revenue discrepancy before investor use, and do not expose respondent names or email addresses.

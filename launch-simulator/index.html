How the model works

1. Comparable baseline
B₍c₎ = median annual units across NL, DK, SE
Exact duplicate records are removed before calculating the home-market baseline.

2. German volume
V = B × G × H × I × (A₍p,c₎ / A₂.₁₉,c) × S × D
G Germany scale, H German channel preference ÷ home channel share, I intent index, A tested acceptance, S seasonality, and D readiness.

3. Economics
Net revenue = V × net price
Contribution = V × unit contribution
Payback = (fixed + marketing spend) / (annual contribution / 12)

4. Positioning & choice
PPI = 100 × (price − mass/niche midpoint) / (premium midpoint − mass/niche midpoint)
The recommendation combines normalized CFO payback/margin and CMO premium-fit scores according to the decision-emphasis slider.

Survey inputs are pre-aggregated from 420 German respondents. Names, emails, respondent IDs, and the raw customer-survey file are never loaded by this site.
